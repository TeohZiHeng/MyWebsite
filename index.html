I'm someone who finds genuine satisfaction in untangling messy data and turning it into something people can actually use — whether that's a dashboard, an automation script, or a five-minute story for a stakeholder who just wants the bottom line.

Outside of spreadsheets and code, I'd describe myself as easygoing but persistent — I don't mind sitting with a hard problem for a while, but I also know when to step back, ask for input, and adjust. I'm also a fairly restless traveller — give me a free weekend and I'll probably be looking at flights. That curiosity about new places mirrors how I approach data: I like exploring first, asking questions later, and only then deciding what the story actually is.
